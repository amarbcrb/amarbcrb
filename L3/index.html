What is marketing?

Marketing is the process of understanding customers, creating value for them, communicating with them, and making products or services available to the right people. It is not only advertising. Tap each card to flip it.

The set of decisions a company makes to offer a product successfully.

Exit questions

Think of your answer first, then open the card to check.

1. What is marketing?Understanding customers, creating value, communicating with them and making products available to the right people.
2. What is the difference between marketing and selling?Selling is one part of the process aimed at a quick sale. Marketing is the whole process aimed at long-term relationships.
3. What are the 4Ps?Product, Price, Place, Promotion.
4. Why is knowing the target market important?It helps the company create, price, promote and sell to the right customers.
5. Give one example of a promotional channel.Social media, TV or online ads, influencers, discounts, loyalty cards.
